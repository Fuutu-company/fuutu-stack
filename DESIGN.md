# Fuutu Stack Design System

The frontmatter above is the contract: token values live there and in `packages/config/brand.css`, and a test keeps the two identical. The prose below explains when and why to use them. It does not restate values.

## Overview

Tactile: a cool canvas, ink-dark primary actions, four pastel product-area colors, pill-shaped controls and soft shadows. Surfaces are white cards that lift off the canvas with a shadow and a faint ring instead of borders. Headings are heavy Schibsted Grotesk; body text is Manrope. Status colors and the area colors are the only hues, so the product reads as one quiet tool with a few friendly accents. It should feel like something you trust with your data and enjoy pressing: calm by default, loud only for destructive or time-critical states.

Mode: product UI first (consistency over expression). Marketing pages are more expressive (larger display type, mesh gradients, the hero bento), but use the same tokens.

## Colors

Colors are semantic. Components never name a palette color; they name a role (`bg-primary`, `text-muted-foreground`, `border-input`). Every surface token has a `-foreground` partner, and that pair is the unit you test and reason about.

- **Ink (oklch(0.24 0.03 265))** · primary actions, selected state, the active sidebar item and the focus ring. Filled buttons are near-black with light text; in dark mode the role flips to near-white oklch(0.95 0.01 265) with dark text.
- **Canvas (oklch(0.965 0.006 240))** and **text (oklch(0.22 0.02 260))** · background and body text. Cards and popovers sit on top as white surfaces separated by shadow.
- **Signal Red (oklch(0.52 0.2 27))** · destructive actions and invalid fields.
- **Success Green (oklch(0.5 0.13 150))** · confirmed and healthy states.
- **Warning Amber (oklch(0.82 0.15 80))** · caution. This is a light fill, so it always carries `warning-foreground` text. It is never used as text color.
- **Info Blue (oklch(0.52 0.12 245))** · neutral notices and hints.
- **Users Blue** · fill oklch(0.9 0.06 230), ink oklch(0.38 0.08 240) · auth, members and organizations.
- **Billing Mint** · fill oklch(0.91 0.07 160), ink oklch(0.38 0.08 160) · plans, invoices and seats.
- **AI Lilac** · fill oklch(0.9 0.055 300), ink oklch(0.4 0.14 300) · chat, credits and usage.
- **API Sand** · fill oklch(0.93 0.08 95), ink oklch(0.42 0.08 85) · API keys, webhooks and MCP.

Area colors mark where you are, never a status. Use `bg-area-<a>` with `text-area-<a>-foreground`; the ink also works as text on background and card. Never put opacity on area text. In dark mode the roles invert: a deep tinted fill (users oklch(0.33 0.06 235), billing oklch(0.33 0.06 160), AI oklch(0.33 0.07 300), API oklch(0.35 0.06 90)) with a light ink.

Supporting roles: `secondary`, `muted` and `accent` are quiet tints for backgrounds and hover; `border` is decorative separation; `input` is the visible edge of a form control and is intentionally stronger than `border`; `ring` is the focus outline (ink, drawn at 50%, audited at 3:1); `chart-1` to `chart-5` are the data series; `sidebar-*` re-skin the navigation shell.

Dark mode is designed, not inverted: surfaces step up in lightness with elevation, and chroma is reduced at the extremes.

## Typography

Schibsted Grotesk for headings and display text (`font-heading`), Manrope for interface and body text (`font-sans`), Geist Mono for code, tokens and identifiers (`font-mono`). The `typography` roles in the frontmatter list the Tailwind classes the app uses; pick the role, do not invent sizes.

- Page titles use `h1`; section titles inside cards or panels use `h2`; list-item titles use `h3`. Headings are heavy: `font-extrabold` for `display` and `h1`, `font-bold` for `h2` and `h3`.
- `display` is the marketing hero. The hero scales fluidly with `text-[clamp(2.75rem,8vw,4.75rem)]`; `text-6xl` is the fixed role size.
- Default reading size is `body`. `small` is for captions, badges and metadata, never for primary content.
- Tighten tracking only on headings (`tracking-heading`, `tracking-display`). Keep body copy at default tracking.
- Fonts are loaded with `next/font/google` in `apps/*/src/app/fonts.ts` (identical in every app, kept in sync by a guard) and exposed through `--font-sans`, `--font-heading` and `--font-mono`. All three are variable fonts and `latin` covers English and German.

## Layout

A 4px base unit. Cards pad and gap at six units, controls are ten units tall (40px; `sm` is 32px and `lg` is 44px), pages use a six-unit gutter. Group related controls tightly and separate groups with a full card gap, so proximity does the work that borders would otherwise do.

The app shell is a floating collapsible sidebar plus a content column. Content is single-column on phones and opens into grids from the `md` breakpoint. Every route must work at 375px and 1280px.

## Elevation & Depth

Cards do not have borders. They sit on the canvas with a soft shadow and a faint foreground ring (`shadow-sm ring-1 ring-foreground/5`), so separation comes from lift, not outline. Form controls keep a 1px `border-input` edge plus an extra-small shadow, because their edge must be perceivable. Overlays (dialog, popover, dropdown) take the larger shadows with a scrim behind them. Do not stack a heavy shadow on a surface that already separates itself.

Shadow tokens come from the theme when a pasted export defines them and fall back to Tailwind defaults otherwise.

## Shapes

One `--radius` drives the whole scale (`sm` 12px to `4xl` 32px at 1rem). Buttons and badges are pills (`rounded-full`), cards use `2xl`, dialogs, sheets and the auth card use `3xl`, inputs and selects use `md`, menu items use `sm`, checkboxes are 4px, avatars and dots are `full`. Changing `--radius` in `brand.css` re-shapes the entire product at once, so choose it as a brand decision, not per component.

## Components

Components come from `@fuutu/ui` (shadcn, new-york style, with the class compositions of the shadcn luma preset ported by hand). The `components` recipes in the frontmatter are the canonical class compositions for the most common pieces, including the `area-tile`.

- Prefer the component over re-assembling its classes.
- Variants express intent: `default` for the one primary action in a view, `secondary` and `outline` for the rest, `ghost` for toolbars, `destructive` only for irreversible actions.
- A status callout is a filled `warning`, `info` or `success` surface with its matching foreground. Destructive confirmations use the alert dialog, not a colored banner.
- Pages open with `PageHeader`. In the SaaS app the breadcrumb already names the page, so app pages use `variant="compact"`: a visually hidden h1 and the page actions in a right-aligned row. Only the dashboard greeting and the `/design` intro show a visible title. Dashboards and landing pages use `AreaTile` for facts; a row of tiles passes `aligned` so every value sits on one baseline. `AreaBackdrop` and `MeshGradient` tint hero surfaces with the area tokens.
- Sidebar icons are plain and identical in size and ink; the active item is the ink pill. No area circles or marker dots in navigation, and no group has an overview item.
- The `/design` page in the SaaS app renders every exported primitive with its states from the live tokens.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do:
- Name a role, not a color: `bg-primary`, `text-muted-foreground`.
- Use one primary button per view.
- Give every icon-only button an accessible name.
- Treat `warning` and `info` as fills, never as text.
- Use an area color to mark the area (a tile, icon or badge), never to signal status.
- Keep body text on `foreground` or `muted-foreground` over `background`, `card` or `popover`.
- Change colors in `brand.css` and DESIGN.md together.

Don't:
- Hard-code Tailwind palette colors (`text-amber-600`, `bg-slate-100`) or hex values in app UI.
- Use `text-warning` or `text-info` on a surface.
- Put opacity on area text (`text-area-ai-foreground/60`); it drops below 4.5:1.
- Rely on color alone to convey state; pair it with an icon or label.
- Remove the focus ring or shrink hit targets below the `sm` control height.
- Add new tokens to `brand.css` outside the shadcn set and the kit status and area blocks.

## Motion

Short and functional: 150 to 250ms for state changes and overlay enter/exit, using the `tw-animate-css` utilities the components already carry. Buttons press to `scale-[0.97]`; the hero and cards enter with a short fade and rise. Motion confirms a change; it is never decoration in product screens. The mesh gradient used on marketing surfaces drifts slowly and stops when its speed is set to zero. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns the press scale and entrance animations off, and anything new must respect it too.

## Iconography

Lucide, outline style, 16px (`size-4`) inside controls and 20 to 24px for standalone icons. Icons inherit `currentColor`, so they follow the text role around them. Icon-only controls need an `aria-label` from the translations.

## Accessibility

Target is WCAG 2.2 AA. Text pairs need 4.5:1; borders of form controls, focus rings and chart series need 3:1 against the background. `pnpm -F @fuutu/ui test` audits every surface/foreground pair in light and dark, including the area fills and inks and the effective focus ring (ring at 50% over background, card and popover), and fails with the pair and mode named. Run it after any token change, including a pasted export. The `/design` page also shows the measured ratio per pair. Focus must always be visible, and all interactive elements must be reachable by keyboard.

## Iteration Guide

Files that move together:

1. `packages/config/brand.css` (values) and this file's frontmatter plus the Colors section (the same values).
2. Fonts: `apps/*/src/app/fonts.ts` (keep the three files identical) and `--font-sans` / `--font-heading` / `--font-mono` in `brand.css`. The root layouts only apply `fontVariables`; never touch `metadata.generator` there.
3. Mail and OG hex: `packages/mail/src/templates/colors.ts`, `packages/config/assets/og-image.html` (re-render and run `pnpm assets:sync`) and the blog `opengraph-image.tsx`.
4. Then run `pnpm -F @fuutu/ui test` and open `/design` in the SaaS app, in light and dark.

Make it calm: set all four area tokens (fill and ink) to one neutral tint, for example `--muted` fills with `--foreground` inks. Tiles, icons and the hero backdrop go quiet and the rest of the product is unchanged. Keep the ink pairs at 4.5:1.

Retuning an area hue: keep the pastel recipe. Light fills sit at lightness 0.9 to 0.93 with low chroma, inks at 0.38 to 0.42; dark fills at 0.33 to 0.35 with inks at 0.88 to 0.9. Change only the hue (and chroma for the ink), then run the audit; every pair must stay at 4.5:1.

Pasting an export from tweakcn or ui.shadcn.com/create:
- Paste only the `:root` and `.dark` blocks into `brand.css`. Do not paste `@import`, `@custom-variant`, `@theme` or `@layer` rules; `theme.css` already handles them.
- Keep the "Kit status tokens" and "Kit area tokens" blocks. Exports do not contain them, and the app needs them.
- Copy the new values into the frontmatter `colors` (and `rounded.lg` if `--radius` changed), update the Colors prose, then run `pnpm -F @fuutu/ui test`. The sync test fails until the two agree; the contrast audit fails if a pair drops below AA.
- Open `/design` and check key routes in both modes.

Guided path: the `setup-brand` skill (Fuutu MCP Plus) interviews you, proposes a direction, computes accessible tokens, applies them and updates this file.

## Known Gaps

Honest list of places that do not yet follow the tokens. Fix these before relying on a pasted theme to reach them.

- **Destructive text is literal white.** Button and Badge `destructive` render `text-white`, so `--destructive-foreground` is ignored. The audit measures white on the destructive fill, which is what users see.
- **Shadows cannot be recolored.** Shadow tokens are mapped with fallbacks, so `shadow-<color>` utilities do not recolor them (they only change when the theme defines `--shadow-*`).
- **Older shadcn class patterns.** Dialog, sheet, select, tabs and switch still use earlier shadcn class compositions. They work with the tokens but differ from current registry output.
- **Email templates use inline hex**, because mail clients do not support CSS variables. `MAIL_COLORS` in `packages/mail/src/templates/colors.ts` holds the light palette: page `#f0f4f7`, card `#ffffff`, border `#d9dfe3`, text `#151b24`, muted `#545b66`, button `#181f2e`, button text `#f7f8fc`. They do not follow a pasted theme; update them by hand.
- **OG images use literal hex.** Social cards can't read CSS variables. The static card is rendered from `packages/config/assets/og-image.html` (light palette, Google Fonts at render time; distributed with `pnpm assets:sync`); the blog card (`apps/marketing/src/app/(marketing)/[locale]/blog/[slug]/opengraph-image.tsx`) hard-codes the light background, text, muted text and the Users Blue pill as hex and renders with the default OG font. The docs app uses Fumadocs' own OG image generator, which does not read the tokens. Re-render or update all of them after a color change.
- **Google logo** keeps its official brand hex values.
- **MacFrame palette** (`packages/ui/src/components/media-frame/constants.ts`) is a fixed set of window-chrome colors, such as the traffic-light dots and titlebar, independent of the theme.
- **State-variant overrides in the SaaS app.** `apps/saas/src/app/globals.css` redefines Tailwind's `hover`, `focus`, `focus-visible` and `active` variants to also match `[data-preview~="…"]`, which pins the states on `/design`. Keep it in mind when debugging specificity.
- **Overlays use `bg-black/80`** for the dialog and sheet scrim, a deliberate exception to the semantic-token rule.
